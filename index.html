FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which AI agents work with Egbe?

Claude Code, Codex, Hermes and OpenClaw. Egbe installs its skills into your project folder, and your agent runs on your own subscription.

How do I connect my agent?

Create a project in the Egbe dashboard. Open your project folder in your agent, give it the project’s Egbe address and key, and ask it to connect. It installs Egbe’s skills and tells you what it can do for your project. The key stays in your project, out of Git.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You talk to your agent in plain language. Egbe’s skills cover the technical steps — hosting, databases, payments — and your agent tells you what it did.

Will my agent spend money or publish anything without asking?

No. Connecting Egbe doesn’t authorize spending, publishing, sending email or launching ads. Your agent works within the scope and budget you approve.

Does Egbe keep working when my agent is off?

Your products stay online on Egbe’s hosting. New work — research, building, campaign changes — happens when your agent runs. If your agent supports scheduled tasks, it can check in on its own.

How do skills stay up to date?

The catalog is versioned. When Egbe releases an update, your agent checks for it and installs it, keeping any local changes you’ve made.

Who owns the product and the code?

You do. Products you build with Egbe are created and owned by you.
