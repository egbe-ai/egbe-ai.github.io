Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Egbe, Inc. ("Egbe", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit egbe.ai, join our waitlist, or use the Egbe platform (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you join our waitlist, request access, contact us, or create an account, we collect details such as your name, email address, company, and anything you choose to include in your message.

Information we collect automatically. When you use the Services, we collect usage and device data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring pages, through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Services;
  • To respond to your inquiries and manage the waitlist and early access;
  • To communicate updates, security notices, and (where permitted) product news;
  • To analyze usage and protect against fraud, abuse, and security risks;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and analytics to understand how the Services are used. We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies and processes usage data on our behalf. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy and opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Most browsers also let you control cookies through their settings.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Services — for example, Google Cloud (hosting), Google Analytics (analytics), and Google Forms (waitlist);
  • Legal and safety recipients, where required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public;
  • Business transfers, if Egbe is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Section 7).

6. International transfers

We operate globally and may process and store information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@egbe.ai and we will respond as required by applicable law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you.
